FAQ

Questions

Is DataForge free?

Yes, everything is free while the app is in beta. Paid plans will follow (see pricing). Join the early-bird list and you keep 40% off for life.

How up to date are the questions?

The question bank is reviewed and refreshed as the exams change. The Professional Data Engineer bank was last updated on {{GCP_PDE_UPDATED}} and the Professional Cloud Architect bank on {{GCP_PCA_UPDATED}}. The app shows the date on every exam, and questions that no longer match the current exam are flagged as outdated.

Do I need an account?

You can try 20 questions per certification without one. An account unlocks the full question bank and saves your progress across devices.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your mobile browser and use “Add to Home Screen” to launch it like an app. A native app is on the roadmap.

Is DataForge affiliated with Google?

No. DataForge is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Google LLC.
